## 🎯 Project Overview
This project focuses on a comprehensive **Sales, Procurement, and Customer Purchase Analysis** for an e-commerce operation specializing in survival and prepping gear. The core objective is to evaluate product performance, understand regional sales distribution, monitor manufacturing vs. retail pricing, and analyze customer behavior across different store segments.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Analysis

### 1. Executive Performance Metrics
* **Total Transactions:** The operations recorded a **Count of 50 Total Purchases**.
* **Volume Sold:** A total of **3,001 units** were sold across major B2B customers and retail stores.
* **Financial Scale:** The overall scale of recorded purchases reached **22K (Sum of Total Purchased)**.

### 2. Product Performance (Top Sellers)
* **Undisputed Leader:** The **Multitool Survival Knife** is the highest-performing product, commanding the largest volume with **477 units sold**.
* **High Demand Essentials:** Core survival utilities follow closely, with **Nylon Rope (390 units)** and **Duct Tape (365 units)** securing the second and third spots.
* **Specialized Gear:** Items like the *Stainless Steel Axe (350 units)* and *N95 Masks (343 units)* maintain consistent sales, while *Solar Battery Flashlights* sit at the lower tier with *182 units*.

### 3. Regional Sales & Distribution
* **Dominant State Market:** **MN (Minnesota)** stands out as the primary revenue and volume driver, contributing a massive **28.14% (6K)** of total purchases.
* **Secondary Hubs:** **NY (New York)** accounts for **17.57% (4K)**, while **IL (Illinois)** represents **12.41% (3K)** of the distribution network.
* **Emerging Territories:** *NC (North Carolina)* and *DE (Delaware)* hold smaller market shares, accounting for *10.4%* and *5.78%* respectively.

### 4. B2B Customer & Segment Breakdown
* **Top Contributing Customer:** **Uncle Joe's Prep Shop** is the most valuable client, acquiring a leading share of **951 total units**.
* **Steady Clients:** *Apocalypse Preppers United* follows with *828 units*, while *Alex The Analyst Apocalypse Preppers (613 units)* and *Prep4Anything Prepping Store (609 units)* show similar purchasing volumes.
* **Product Mix Behavior:** The *Customer Purchase Breakdown* reveals distinct behaviors. For instance, *Alex The Analyst's* orders are highly dominated by *Multitool Survival Knives (31%)*, whereas *Prep4Anything Prepping Store* relies heavily on *Duct Tape (39.57%)*.

### 5. Financial & Cost Dynamics (Margin Analysis)
* **Pricing vs. Cost Structure:** The *Sum of Price and Sum of Production Cost* chart tracks product margins. High-value weatherproofing jackets and specialized gear maintain a healthy gap between retail price and cost of production, maximizing net profitability per unit.
